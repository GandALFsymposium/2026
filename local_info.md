#### Conference Venue

GandALF 2026 will take place at Hotel [Comwell Hvide Hus Aalborg](https://www.comwell.com/hoteller/comwell-hotel-aalborg), 
conveniently located in the heart of Aalborg, next to the city's central park, **Kildeparken**. 
The venue is within easy walking distance (approximately 9 minutes) of **Aalborg Station**, 
the city's main railway and bus station, via a pleasant route through the park.

The conference sessions will be held in **Room M2**, located on the ground floor of the hotel. 
The room is equipped with a projector, projection screen, and a small whiteboard.

The projector supports a standard HDMI connection. A selection of adapters for common 
video connectors will be available in the conference room; however, we recommend that 
presenters bring any adapters they may require for their own devices.


#### Travel Options

<div class="row">
<div class="col-lg-4" markdown="1">

###### Plane

Aalborg is easily accessible by air, with frequent daily flights from Copenhagen and 
regular direct connections from Amsterdam and Frankfurt.

[Aalborg Airport](https://aal.dk/) is located approximately 7 km from the city centre. 
From the airport, participants can reach the conference venue either by taxi 
(approximately 10–15 minutes) or by public transport, using bus line **12** or a train 
to **Aalborg Station**, the city's main railway and bus station.
</div>

<div class="col-lg-4" markdown="1">

###### Train or Bus

Aalborg is well connected by train and long-distance bus services from/to Copenhagen, 
Aarhus, and many other European cities.

For train travel within Denmark and local bus connections to Aalborg, we recommend 
planning your journey using [Rejseplanen](https://www.rejseplanen.dk/webapp-nextgen/). 
Tickets can be purchased either at the train station or through the 
[Rejsebillet app](https://www.rejsebillet.dk/en-us), available for both iOS and Android 
(payment is by credit/debit card, using the app).

For longer coach journeys (e.g. from Copenhagen or other European cities), we recommend 
using [FlixBus](https://www.flixbus.com/).

</div>
<div class="col-lg-4" markdown="1">

###### Automobile

We strongly encourage participants to use public transportation whenever possible.

Free public parking near the conference venue is very limited, and many parking spaces 
are restricted to a maximum stay of one hour. 
Parking permits for visitors may be available at **Hotel Comwell Hvide Hus Aalborg**; 
however, participants interested in this option should contact the hotel directly 
in advance to inquire about availability.
</div>
</div>
